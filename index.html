My Biography

Hey, I’m Laurel, just a young girl constantly thinking about the future she’s trying to build for herself amidst all the uncertainties of today’s world. I’m an undergraduate who jumped at the opportunity to explore a field that has always seemed so foreign to me. I study Accounting, a far cry from Software Engineering, but somehow, the difference frightens, yet excites me.

I’ve always struggled with knowing what I want to be in the future, other than successful. A few months ago, I realized there is more to life than simply operating within structures that already exist. You’re allowed to explore, create, and dream. Now, that girl born in 2006 and raised in Jos, pursuing an Accounting degree, has found something beautiful to chase: something with no limits. And even though this engineering degree doesn’t directly relate to what I’ve chosen to become, it is closely connected to where I want to go, and I believe it will help me get there.

Why I Joined the Program

Truthfully, it wasn’t because I had an earnest desire to become a software engineer. I joined because I saw it as an opportunity to learn the basics and intermediate parts of coding that I could eventually use to explore game development with tools like Unity, Unreal Engine, and Ren’Py. I came across ALT School by chance through my school’s WhatsApp TV, and the fact that there was an ongoing scholarship, meaning I didn’t have to pay, made it an even better opportunity.

Regardless, I’m genuinely happy to be here. Along the way, I’ve discovered that I actually enjoy building things, whether that’s games, software, or really anything that can exist on the web.

My Goals For Joining the Program

  1. To learn the basics of coding and software development.
  2. To explore game development and create my own games.
  3. To gain experience in the tech industry and build a career in game development and design.
  4. To create an alternative source of income.

That's basically it, but plans and goals are liable to change, so I'll see where the next 12 months takes me.😉
