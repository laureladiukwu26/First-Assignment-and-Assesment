About Chatter

Chatter is a multi-functional platform where authors and readers can have access to their own content. It aims to be a traditional bookworm's haven and a blog to get access to more text-based content. Our vision is to foster an inclusive and vibrant community where diversity is celebrated. We encourage open-mindedness and respect for all individuals, regardless of their background and beliefs.

Why you should join chatter

Our goal is to make writers and readers see our platform as their next haven for blogging, ensuring ease in interactions, connecting with like-minded peers, have access to favourite content based on interests and able to communicate your great ideas to people.

Analytics

Analytics to track the number of views, likes and comments and also analyze the performance of your articles over a period of time.

Social Interactions

Users on the platform can interact with posts they like, comment, and engage in discussions.

Content Creation

Write nice and appealing with our in-built markdown, a rich text editor.
